## Continue rather than restart

A02 extends the first Breakout exercise. The original instruction is to follow parts 6–12 of the reference tutorial and continue further if time allows. The task is to integrate new work into an existing program rather than begin with an empty file each time.

The reference resources are the same external materials used in A01:

- [YouTube reference playlist](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLea8cjCua_P0qjjiG8G5FBgqwpqMU7rBk)
- [Bilibili: making Breakout with PICO-8](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV17rAieaEmB/)

These videos have not been reviewed episode by episode for this page, so it does not claim a verified checklist of their individual contents.

## Working method

Continue from a runnable A01 version and state what you intend to add. If the new behavior breaks movement or boundary responses, retain the failing case and inspect it incrementally instead of rewriting the whole program.

The edited guidance asks for three observations: what changed since A01, one implementation you can explain clearly, and one aspect that remains unclear or faulty. This makes subsequent support more useful than simply reporting that every video was watched.

## Submission

Submit `A02_Name.zip` containing:

- `A02_Name.p8.png`: the extended cartridge.
- `A02_Name.mp4`: a demonstration and explanation under one minute.

The original A02 handout accidentally retains A01 names for its inner files. This version consistently uses A02 to prevent confusion or overwriting. Explain your modifications, identifying methods drawn from the tutorial when relevant.

## Self-check and feedback

Recheck the basic A01 behavior before showing new work. For an error, provide repeatable steps so a classmate or assistant can reproduce it. For an extension, explain which experience it serves and what you deferred to make it manageable.

These questions are edited exercise guidance, not a claim that complete scoring and statistical records exist. The assignment supports independent modification and finishing a small scope before the [game jam](/teaching/pico8-game-jam/).
