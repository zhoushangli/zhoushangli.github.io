## About the jam

After the PICO-8 training, the game club held a week-long game jam. Participants chose their own mechanics, built prototypes and explored revisions through playtest feedback.

The gallery below shows PNG covers retained from the activity. The games belong to their respective creators. [Return to the training overview](/teaching/pico8-2026/).
