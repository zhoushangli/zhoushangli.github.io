## About the training

I was the main instructor for four introductory PICO-8 sessions: an Intro and three main lessons, about 90 minutes each. Classes combined explanations and follow-along coding, with Q&A and exercises afterwards. Teaching assistants and other contributors also supported the course. A week-long club game jam followed.

## Course outline

| Session | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Intro | Get to know PICO-8, save and run projects, and introduce the course and jam |
| L01 | Drawing, variables, button input and character movement |
| L02 | Debugging, movement functions and animation |
| L03 | Object management, maps and level creation |

The supporting Breakout exercises cover ball movement, boundary collisions and sound, followed by further development of the game.
